2. Information we process

2.1 Account information

When you create or use a HandyPet account, we may process:

  • your email address and display name
  • your account identifier
  • your sign-in method
  • account creation and login information; and

authentication and account-security information.

Authentication is provided using Google Firebase Authentication.

If you use Google, Apple, or another available third-party sign-in option, that provider may provide the information needed to authenticate your account. HandyPet does not receive your third-party account password.

2.2 Pet profiles and pet-care information

You may choose to store information about your pets, including:

  • pet name, species, breed, birth date, sex, weight, color, microchip number, and profile photo
  • vaccinations and vaccination dates
  • veterinary visits, dates, clinic or veterinarian names, costs, and notes
  • medications, dosage, frequency, dates, conditions, refill information, and notes
  • medical records
  • reminders
  • weight history; and

documents and files you choose to upload.

Pet-care information relates primarily to animals and is not intended to contain human medical information.

Please do not include personal information about another person unless you are authorized to provide it.

2.3 Photos, documents, and PDF exports

HandyPet accesses only photos and files that you choose through your device's available file or photo selection tools.

Pet profile photos may be stored locally on your device.

Documents that you choose to upload may be stored using Firebase Storage so that they can be associated with your HandyPet account.

PDF health-record exports are generated for your use. They leave HandyPet only when you choose to save, print, export, or share them using your device or another application.

Once you share or export a file to another application or service, that third party's privacy practices apply to its handling of the file.

2.4 Settings and reminders

HandyPet may store preferences such as:

  • application language
  • measurement system
  • appearance or theme
  • notification preferences
  • reminder settings; and

other application preferences.

Reminder information may be provided to your device's notification system so that notifications can be displayed.

Depending on your device settings, notification content may be visible on the lock screen.

2.5 Subscription and purchase information

HandyPet offers paid subscription features.

Subscriptions and payments are processed through the applicable app store, such as Apple App Store or Google Play, and are managed with RevenueCat.

To provide and manage subscriptions, we and RevenueCat may process information such as:

  • an App User ID or other identifier associated with your HandyPet account
  • product, package, plan, and entitlement identifiers
  • subscription status
  • purchase and renewal information
  • trial or paid status
  • expiration and renewal dates
  • purchase history
  • Apple purchase receipts or Google Play purchase tokens
  • billing or subscription issue status; and

limited technical information needed to operate the subscription service.

RevenueCat uses this information to validate purchases, determine subscription entitlements, restore purchases, prevent subscription abuse, and provide subscription and purchase reporting.

We do not send your pet-care records, medical documents, uploaded pet files, or pet notes to RevenueCat.

Payment credentials such as your full credit-card or bank-account information are processed by the applicable app store or payment provider. HandyPet and RevenueCat do not receive your complete payment-card details from App Store or Google Play purchases.

3. Technical information

Our service providers may automatically process limited technical information necessary to operate, secure, and maintain their services.

This may include information such as:

  • IP address
  • device or operating-system information
  • application information
  • request timestamps
  • authentication events
  • security events; and

technical information generated when a request or transaction fails.

HandyPet does not sell personal information.

HandyPet does not use personal information for targeted advertising or cross-app advertising tracking.

HandyPet does not use pet-care information or uploaded documents for advertising.

We do not currently use advertising SDKs or general-purpose third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. RevenueCat provides subscription and purchase reporting as part of its subscription-management service.

4. How we use information

We use information where necessary to:

  • create and manage your HandyPet account
  • authenticate users and protect accounts
  • save, synchronize, restore, display, export, and delete pet-care information
  • provide uploaded-document storage
  • provide reminders and apply your settings
  • provide and manage HandyPet Pro subscriptions
  • validate purchases and restore subscription access
  • prevent fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, and subscription misuse
  • troubleshoot technical problems
  • maintain the reliability and security of the Service
  • respond to support and privacy requests; and

comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not use pet-care records or uploaded documents for automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

7. Local storage, cloud storage, and international processing

HandyPet may store settings and working copies of information locally on your device.

Account information and pet-care records associated with a signed-in account may be synchronized with Cloud Firestore.

Documents you choose to upload may be stored using Firebase Storage.

Some information may therefore be processed on servers located outside your country of residence, including in the United States or other locations where our service providers operate infrastructure.

Where applicable data-protection law requires safeguards for international transfers, our service providers may use recognized data-transfer mechanisms and applicable data-processing agreements.

Information stored locally on your device may remain there until it is deleted by HandyPet, you clear application storage, uninstall the application, or your operating system removes the data.

Device or cloud backups controlled by your operating-system provider may retain copies according to that provider's own backup and retention policies.

Deletion does not necessarily remove:

  • information that Apple, Google, or another app-store provider is legally or operationally required to retain

To prevent future renewal charges, you must cancel the subscription using the subscription-management options provided by the applicable app store.

Account deletion and subscription cancellation are separate actions.
