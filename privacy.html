2. Information we process

2.1 Account and identity information

When you create or use an account, we may process:

  • your email address and display name;
  • your Firebase user identifier;
  • the sign-in method associated with your account;
  • account creation and last-login timestamps;
  • onboarding and account-state information; and
  • authentication and account-security events processed by our authentication provider.

If you use Google or another supported sign-in provider, that provider sends the account information required to authenticate you. We do not receive your third-party account password.

2.2 Pet profiles and pet-care records

You may choose to provide:

  • pet name, species, breed, birth date, sex, weight, color, microchip number, and profile photo;
  • vaccination names, dates, batch numbers, due dates, and notes;
  • veterinary visit reasons, dates, times, veterinarian or clinic names, costs, currencies, completion status, and notes;
  • medication names, conditions, dosage, frequency, dates, prescriber names, refill information, and notes;
  • medical-record titles, types, dates, veterinarian or clinic names, costs, currencies, and notes;
  • reminder titles, dates, times, completion status, sources, and notes;
  • weight measurements and notes; and
  • document names, types, sizes, dates, notes, storage references, and files you select for upload.

Please do not include personal information about another person unless you are authorized to do so.

2.3 Files, photos, and exports

Handy Pet accesses only the photos and files you choose through the operating system picker.

In the current implementation:

  • pet profile photo files remain on your device and are not uploaded to our cloud storage;
  • selected pet-document files may be uploaded to Firebase Storage when cloud document storage is used; and
  • health-record PDFs are generated locally. A PDF leaves Handy Pet only when you choose to save, print, or share it through another application or service.

2.4 Settings and notification information

We process app language, measurement system, theme, notification preferences, default reminder timing, permission-state markers, and locally dismissed warnings. Scheduled reminder content is provided to your operating system so it can display local notifications.

Depending on your device settings, notification text may be visible on the lock screen. Handy Pet does not currently use Firebase Cloud Messaging to send these reminders.

2.5 Subscription and purchase information

If Handy Pet Pro billing is enabled, we may receive purchase and entitlement information such as:

  • product, base-plan, offer, and package identifiers;
  • subscription status, billing period, trial or paid status, renewal state, expiration date, and purchase history needed to restore access;
  • app-store transaction identifiers or purchase tokens; and
  • billing-issue status supplied by the purchase platform.

The applicable app store processes your payment credentials. We do not receive or store your complete card or bank-account details.

Stage 2 review: RevenueCat is planned but is not currently installed in the application. Before production billing launches, confirm the exact data RevenueCat receives, add RevenueCat to Section 6, and align this policy with the RevenueCat project settings and account-deletion workflow.

2.6 Technical information

Our cloud and store providers may automatically process technical information needed to provide and protect their services, such as IP address, device or app information, request timestamps, authentication logs, security events, and diagnostic information generated when a request fails.

Handy Pet does not currently include advertising SDKs, third-party analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs. We do not use your information for cross-app tracking or targeted advertising.

3. How we collect information

We collect information:

  • directly from you when you create an account or enter, select, upload, export, or update content;
  • automatically from the app when it synchronizes or performs account, security, notification, or subscription operations; and
  • from authentication providers, app stores, and purchase infrastructure when you use those services.

4. Why we use information

We use information to:

  • create, authenticate, secure, and manage your account;
  • save, display, synchronize, restore, export, and delete your pet records;
  • schedule local reminders and apply your settings;
  • provide, verify, restore, and manage Pro access;
  • prevent fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, and subscription bypass;
  • troubleshoot failures and maintain the reliability and security of the Service;
  • respond to support, privacy, and legal requests; and
  • comply with applicable law and enforce our Terms of Use.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use pet-care records or uploaded documents for advertising, profiling, or automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

6. Service providers and disclosures

We disclose information only as needed for the purposes described above:

  • Google Firebase provides authentication, Cloud Firestore database, and Firebase Storage services. See Firebase Privacy and Security.
  • Google Sign-In may authenticate users who choose that option. Google's own privacy terms apply to its direct relationship with you.
  • Google Play processes Android purchases and subscription management. We receive purchase status rather than full payment-card details.
  • Apple and the App Store, if the iOS version is distributed with in-app purchases, may process authentication, distribution, purchases, and subscription management under Apple's policies.
  • RevenueCat, if enabled in Stage 2, will process app-user identifiers and purchase or entitlement data on our behalf. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.
  • Professional advisers, authorities, or transaction counterparties may receive limited information where reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights and safety, obtain professional advice, or complete a merger, financing, acquisition, or sale subject to appropriate safeguards.

Our processors are contractually or legally required to protect information consistent with their role and applicable law. We do not authorize service providers to use Your Content for their own advertising.

7. Local storage and cloud synchronization

Handy Pet stores app settings and working copies of pet data on your device so the app can function responsively and support offline use. Signed-in account data and pet-care records are mirrored to Cloud Firestore. Selected pet documents may also be stored in Firebase Storage.

Local data can remain on a device until you delete it, clear app storage, uninstall the app, sign out where the app clears account data, or delete your account. Operating-system backups may retain app data according to your device and backup settings.

8. International data transfers

We and our service providers may process information outside your country. Firebase Authentication is operated from United States data centers, while Cloud Firestore and Firebase Storage may use Google infrastructure in selected or global locations depending on project configuration.

Where required, we use contractual or other recognized safeguards for international transfers. Contact us for information about safeguards that apply to your data.

9. Retention

We retain account and cloud pet data while your account is active and as needed to provide the Service. Individual records remain until you delete them or delete your account.

When account deletion completes, Handy Pet deletes the account's active Firebase Authentication record, Cloud Firestore records, uploaded Firebase Storage document files, and app-managed local account data on that device. Deletion may not remove:

  • information the applicable app store must retain for purchases, refunds, tax, fraud prevention, or legal compliance;
  • information retained where we have a legal obligation or need it to resolve disputes or enforce agreements; or
  • transient copies in processor backups or caches until overwritten under the provider's ordinary retention cycle.

Any retained information will be limited to the purpose requiring retention and deleted or anonymized when that purpose ends.
